Privacy Policy

WanderFinder · Hidden Objects World Tour · Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: WanderFinder does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

WanderFinder has no sign-in and no user accounts. The app does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings (completed levels, postcards, stamps, souvenirs, coins, volume and language) are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are removed if you delete the app.
  • A small anonymous play log (for example how long a level took and where taps landed) is kept on your device to help balance level difficulty. It contains no name, contact details or device identifiers, and it never leaves your device on its own. You can choose to share it with us from Settings; nothing is sent unless you do that yourself.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location once to show where you start from as a dot on the in-game world map. Your location is used only on your device at that moment. It is never saved, uploaded or shared. You can decline or turn this off at any time in iOS Settings; the game works the same without it.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as extra hints or removing the ad space) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

WanderFinder is suitable for all ages. Because the app does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children.

Changes

If a future version of WanderFinder changes how data is handled (for example by adding advertising), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Contact
