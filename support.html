WanderFinder Support

Hidden Objects World Tour

Thanks for travelling with Poco! If something isn't working, or you have an idea, write to us at support@wanderfinder.app. Please include your device model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Is there a timer? Can I fail a level?
No. There are no timers and no lives. Take your time, zoom in and explore.

I ran out of hints.
You get fresh hints in every new country. You can also buy extra hints in the shop; they never expire and work in every country.

How do I restore my "Remove ads" purchase on a new device?
Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases".

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. You can play offline.
